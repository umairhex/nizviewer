## NizViewer v1.5.2

### Highlights

- Added detection for email-based application instructions in job descriptions.
- Added an Apply by email action with the recipient and requested subject pre-filled.
- Included application email and subject fields in copied and exported job data.
- Removed the unused Compare workflow from cards, toolbar, state, styles, and public documentation.
- Preserved full-detail fetching, filtering, sorting, retry states, and CSV export.

### Validation

- ESLint and web-ext lint pass with no errors.
- Chrome and Firefox extension builds pass.
