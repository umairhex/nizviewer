Complete guide

Make NizViewer fit your search style.

First-time setup

  1. Install NizViewer and open any Indeed page.
  2. Keep the default Light theme and Always expanded density, or open Settings to choose your preference.
  3. Use the date filters to hide old listings or adjust the freshness window.
  4. Search normally; cards update as Indeed adds results.

When a detail is missing

  1. Read the status on the card: queued, loading, complete, or failed.
  2. Use Retry when Indeed temporarily limits a request.
  3. Open the original view-job link for the canonical listing.
  4. Refresh only if the page itself has become stale; your saved preferences remain local.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does it work on search results and direct view-job links?

Yes. NizViewer observes Indeed landing pages, search result cards, and /viewjob?jk=... pages so the same information is available whichever route you take.

Will long titles or locations take over the card?

No. Result-card job titles, company names, and locations stay capped at two lines for scanning, while the standalone description remains scrollable and complete.

What happens when a request fails?

The card keeps the information already available, shows a clear status, and offers a retry instead of silently replacing the content.

Is my data sent somewhere?

NizViewer is local-first. It stores preferences in browser storage and requests job details from the Indeed pages you are already visiting.
