## NizViewer v1.5.3

### Highlights

- Detects phone-based application instructions in full job descriptions.
- Adds an Apply by phone action that opens the detected number through the device phone handler.
- Keeps email application actions and requested subjects available on job cards.
- Captures application contacts from direct fetches, standalone view-job pages, and page-hook payloads.
- Includes application contact fields in copied and exported job data.
- Adds false-positive protection for salary, payment, commission, target, date, and unrelated contact text.
- Refreshes older cached listings with detail scan version 4.

### Validation

- ESLint and web-ext lint pass with no errors.
- Chrome and Firefox extension builds pass.
